# SERP notes, decor textiles
## best-lumbar-pillows
- Guides mix office and couch lumbar supports; couch picks emphasize ventilated memory foam and dual straps.
- Shape matters: D shaped for deep couches, rolls for firm upright seats.
- Picks split by budget, clinical style, and cover material (mesh, washable).
- Several warn that stat claims on back pain relief lack methodology.
- Gap we cover: sofa seat depth vs pillow thickness, and honest no medical claims.

## best-weighted-blankets
- Guides lead with weight as a percent of body weight (about 10 percent) and offer weights from about 7 to 30 lb.
- Picks split by cooling, cotton, sherpa, luxury, value and by size.
- Common warnings: many run hot, beaded fill is rarely machine washable so a removable cover helps.
- Health claims are described as limited evidence.
- Gap we cover: throw vs queen size, pocket size and bead migration, and washer capacity.

## best-chunky-knit-blankets
- Roundups lead with yarn type (chenille, wool, cotton) and machine washability.
- Picks split by budget, wool, lightweight, weighted and size.
- Care notes: gentle cycle, air dry, loose weaves may not be washable.
- Open knits breathe but insulate less.
- Gap we cover: snag risk with cats and jewelry, and shedding claims we cannot verify.

## best-coasters
- Guides agree porous materials absorb most: sandstone, cork, felt, pulpboard and cotton.
- Ceramic is called less absorbent than stone, suitable for light spills.
- Picks split by most absorbent, solid build (sandstone), dishwasher safe and style.
- Cold drink condensation is the main use case; sandstone or cork performs best.
- Gap we cover: heat vs condensation tradeoff, table material pairing and honest absorbency claims.

## best-heated-throw-blankets
- Roundups name cordless battery throws, Sunbeam plug in models, smart Wi-Fi throws and dual control picks.
- Buying advice: washability (wiring and batteries limit it), portability, automatic shut-off, dual zone for couples.
- Running cost is modest, about 50 to 200 W.
- Budget picks sit under about 50 dollars in lists; splurge picks are dual control.
- Gap we cover: certification marks vs maker test claims, throw vs bed size and never folding while on.
