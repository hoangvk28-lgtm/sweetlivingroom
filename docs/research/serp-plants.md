## best-hanging-planters
- Roundups pick overall, budget macrame hangers, modern pairs and self-watering designs with a detachable base; many decorative hangers have no drainage holes.
- Advice: choose pots with drainage, or use a liner or self-watering design; small herb planters suit 3 inch pots.
- We add wet-weight fixings, ceiling hook rating, algae in reservoirs and macrame fraying.
## best-indoor-planters
- Drainage is the top factor; clay suits dry plants, ceramic holds water longer; pebble layers are a stopgap.
- Picks: white ceramic with drainage, budget saucer pots, wood buckets and large self-watering pots for big plants.
- We add pot size vs root ball, saucer fit, ceramic and concrete weight and glaze finish.
## best-grow-lights-for-houseplants
- Experts favour LEDs; simple white spectrum LEDs suit most houseplants; start about two feet away; succulents and orchids want stronger light.
- Picks span tabletop, kits and stylish stand lights.
- We add distance falloff, timers, bulb vs bar vs ring and honest lumen claims.
