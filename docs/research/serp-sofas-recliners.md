## best-velvet-sofas
- Top guides split picks by use: best overall, families (performance velvet), budget, custom colour, small space loveseat, modular.
- They lead with fabric type (performance vs standard velvet), then arm and back shape (curved, tufted, track arm).
- Modular and curved shapes keep appearing; delivery and doorway fit is an angle that is easy to answer.
- Many mix price-in-text; we skip that. Gap: crush marks, pile direction and pet hair behaviour are rarely explained.
## best-power-recliners
- Top guides separate lift chairs for mobility from everyday power recliners; splitting picks that way is common.
- Lead criteria: motor count, wall hugger clearance, USB and battery options, leather vs fabric.
- Specialty picks: big and tall, glider and nursery, theater, budget.
- Gap: cord routing, outlet position and battery pack caveats are rarely covered; add an honest power-failure note.
## best-floor-chairs
- Top guides pick by use: best overall memory foam, value mesh, swivel, portable folding, gaming.
- They stress back support limits and that floor chairs suit short sessions; some include swivel and gaming types.
- Lead with padding thickness and number of back angles; portability comes up often.
- Gap: knee and hip strain at floor height and lock wear are rarely covered; we add both.
## best-chesterfield-sofas

- Sizing and whether the sofa is firm come up; few state seat depth.
- Gap: PU leather peeling, feather seat plumping and door-fit are rarely covered; we add them.
## best-mid-century-modern-sofas
- Top guides pick best overall, statement, budget, leather, sectional, small-space loveseat.
- Lead with wood frame, tapered legs and tailored upholstery; some include modulars.
- Vintage designers (Wormley, Pearsall) are cited as authentic alternatives.
- Gap: mid-century is a style label not a standard; honest note on engineered frames and leg bolts added.
## best-sleeper-chairs
- Top guides split by style (modern, midcentury, armless compact) and price (budget under 300 to premium).
- Key choice they frame: true pull-out vs futon-style chair; check size in chair and bed mode.
- They note bed is narrower and shorter than twin; best for short stays.
- Gap: honest bed length vs height of sleeper and floor-level boneless tradeoff; we cover both.
## best-kids-sofas
- Search returned retail listings and roundups: budget 2-in-1 toddler fold-out loungers for ages 1 to 4, sleeper sofas for kids (Serta-style) used as guest beds, premium modular play couches that convert into forts.
- Star ratings and review counts were the only quality signal on retail pages; durability and fabric washability matter more.
- Gap: stated open size and seat height, honest foam compression, and maker-stated certification (CPC) vs vague safety words; we cover all three.

## best-settees
- Guides define a settee (50 to 90 in wide, small sofa for two), lead with size and small-space use, and flag settees as often decorative.
- Splits: comfort picks, value, classic tufted Chesterfield style, entryway settee benches.
- Criteria: hardwood frame, cushion thickness, wipe-clean faux leather for families.
- We add arm-less seat-width trap, settee vs loveseat vs bench distinction and seat-depth honesty.

## best-curved-sofas
- Guides split by sofa vs sectional, velvet vs boucle, budget and small spaces; statement pieces called out.
- Common angles: curved vs straight sofa choice and room layout.
- We add floating-versus-wall placement, wedge gap rule, boneless vs framed tradeoff and foam softening in year two.
