## best-indoor-security-cameras
- Roundups name Ring, Blink, Google Nest, Arlo and Tapo or eufy as picks; outlets disagree on the top spot and Which? and others test dozens.
- Lead criteria: video quality, ease of setup, privacy and hacking risk, subscription cost, storage options.
- Picks split by Alexa, Google or HomeKit support and by budget; pan tilt and no subscription lists are common.
- Subscription lock ins (person alerts, history) are the most cited surprise.
- Gap we cover: local microSD vs cloud tradeoffs, plug in power loss, privacy zones and placement rules, honest limits of AI detection claims.
