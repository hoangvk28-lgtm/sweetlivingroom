## best-barrel-chairs
- Top guides pick best overall, value, swivel, modern, small-space and statement oversized shapes.
- They lead with swivel vs fixed, fabric (boucle, linen, faux leather) and pair vs single.
- Several sell sets of 2; seat width and depth are rarely given.
- Gap: seat width vs overall width and loop-fabric snagging; we add both.
## best-wingback-chairs
- Top guides pick by style: classic, modern, recliner, rocker, upholstered budget, leather.
- They lead with back height, seat firmness and fabric; some mention wing function for drafts.
- Dimensions are often missing; recliner wingbacks appear in some lists.
- Gap: seat height vs rising ease and listing-spec gaps; we add both.
## best-rocking-chairs
- Top guides split picks by use: porch rocker, wooden classic, nursery glider, portable, wicker, rocker-recliner.
- They lead with material (oak, wicker), indoor vs outdoor suitability and glider vs rocker.
- Nursery and glider picks recur; recliner-rocker combos appear.
- Gap: runner clearance, rug fringe and toe safety, joint loosening; we add all three.
## best-papasan-chairs
- Top guides pick best overall swivel wicker, value swivel, indoor and outdoor, budget saucer, boucle.
- They lead with swivel, cushion thickness and weight rating; note papasans are poor for long sitting.
- Cushion-only listings pollute results.
- Gap: cushion migration, bowl edge loading and rising ease; we cover all three.
## best-footstools
- Top guides split by use: all-round, storage, budget, outdoor, upholstered cushioned.
- They lead with height vs seat, weight capacity and storage; many include pouffes.
- Small-space guides stress multi-use (seat, table).
- Gap: matching height to the seat and fold-flat seam wear; we state both honestly.
## best-rattan-chairs
- Search returned editorial roundups (Apartment Therapy lists such as Safavieh, CB2 and a Venice rattan chair that can move outdoors; Livingetc's resort-style list) and retail listings of Safavieh and bali and pari chairs.
- Buying notes: natural rattan suits indoors or covered spaces and dislikes direct rain and prolonged sun; sit in a chair for at least five minutes to find pressure points.
- Gap: frame under the weave, firm bare-woven seats and pet snagging; we cover all three.

## best-floor-cushions
- Guides lead with fill (shredded memory foam), thickness after expansion (5 inch for heavier users), washable covers and set-of-two value.
- Splits: meditation, reading nook, boho style, kids and classroom.
- We add compression recovery, round vs square footprint, why kids sets are not adult seating, and cover zipper quality.
