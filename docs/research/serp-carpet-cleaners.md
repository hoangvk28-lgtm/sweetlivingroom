## best-carpet-cleaners
- Top guides name Hoover SmartWash+ best upright (easiest to operate), Bissell ProHeat for pets, Bissell Big Green for whole-house, Hoover PowerDash Pet for compact and value, Shark CarpetXpert with sprayer.
- They split by pets, compact, whole house; weight and storage footprint come up.
- Large machines are heavy and hard to store.
- Gap: over-wetting and the fact that onboard heat is not true steam; we cover both.
## best-carpet-cleaning-solutions
- Top guides: Bissell Pro Max Clean plus Protect often best overall and best for machines; enzymes for pets; OxiClean as value; Puracy for natural.
- They lead with pets and odours, machine compatibility and stain types; DIY vinegar mixes are common.
- Cautions: test a hidden patch, rinse with plain water to avoid soap buildup.
- Gap: oxy vs enzyme chemistry and scent masking odour; we separate the two.
## best-robot-vacuums-for-carpet
- Top guides: RTINGS names Roborock or Dreame as best for carpet and eufy for budget; Digital Trends picks Roborock Q7 Max plus and S7 MaxV Ultra for thick carpet and notes Roomba j7+ is weaker on deep carpet.
- Buying tips: auto-detect rugs, boost suction, lift the mop, handle high pile; low and mid pile need less power.
- Vendor blogs favour their own models.
- Gap: peak suction numbers are not comparable and thick shag defeats robots; we say both.
## best-cordless-vacuums
- Top guides lead with Dyson models (V11 Animal, V15 Detect), Shark Stratos for upper mid-range, Samsung Jet 75, Tineco Pure ONE S11 as value (about nine pounds, poor for stairs), Black+Decker for small spaces and Shark pet models.
- Caveats: cordless vacuums trade suction and features for convenience; budget runtimes about 24 to 40 minutes; max power can drain a battery in about 10 minutes.
- Gap: honest runtime by mode and the fact that kPa figures are not comparable; we cover both.

## best-upholstery-cleaners
- Reviewers split upholstery cleaners into spray or foam solutions and portable steam or extraction machines; wipes and vacuums are sometimes listed.
- Portable extractors from major carpet brands recur as top picks; budget foams and premium sprays anchor the low end.
- Material warnings repeat: avoid harsh chemicals or machines on leather, velvet and suede; keep microfiber away from heavy sprays.
- Several lists mix UK and US products and span 2022 to 2026.
- Gap: the W, S, WS and X cleaning code tags, over-wetting foam, and the water ring after a spot clean.
