# SERP notes, pet furniture
## best-cat-trees-that-look-like-furniture
- Guides name a 62 in modern tree with a natural wood look as best overall, plus premium wooden models in walnut and oak.
- Features praised: removable machine washable cushions, non-slip pads, furniture grade build.
- One furniture grade pick has no condos or enclosed areas; plush fabric and rattan style accents are warmer than carpet.
- Some picks are large and expensive for multi cat homes.
- Gap we cover: weight limits, anchoring and inconsistent listing heights.
