## best-linen-curtains
- Recurring picks: H.VERSAILTEX, NICETOWN, Nanspring, MIULEE (a 30% linen blend), Joydeco long panels, and customisable Belgian flax from specialists.
- Guides disagree on specs (blackout versus semi-sheer), so fibre content and lining must be checked; denser weave or lining insulates better.
- Gap: real linen content versus linen-look polyester, wrinkling and shrinkage, ring and header choice and light filtering honesty; we cover these.
## best-velvet-curtains
- NICETOWN black velvet blackout is the top pick (300 GSM claim); MIULEE olive is the budget pick with 70 to 80 percent light blocking and not true blackout.
- Advice: check length for low ceilings, vacuum with a soft brush attachment every few weeks.
- Gap: pile crush marks, dye transfer, weight on the rod, and light leak at the header; we cover these.
## best-roman-shades
- Blinds.com Premium, Chicology, Arlo Blinds, Levolor (motorised) and Loom Decor recur; cordless is repeatedly recommended.
- Guides split by room (bedroom lined or blackout, kitchen hardy fabric, bathroom thermal) and by flat versus hobbled folds.
- Gap: inside versus outside mount sizing, lift mechanism wear, fold stacking height that blocks the glass; we cover these.
## best-bamboo-blinds
- Arlo Blinds, LazBlinds no-drill, BAYUE budget, EVOZEN indoor-outdoor and Chicology recur.
- Advice: cordless for child safety, lining for privacy, matchstick styles let in more light than flat slats.
- Gap: fade and brittleness in sun, valance and stack height, moisture and mould in bathrooms, and honest light filtering; we cover these.
