## best-vases
- Guides sort by material (ceramic, glass, brass and metal, stoneware) and by use: small bouquets, minimalist, statement or tall pieces.
- Advice: match material and height to room and flowers; glass reflects light in bright rooms; tall vases suit open spaces.
- We add the 1.5 times height rule, glazed interior check, sightline height and weight for stability.
## best-floor-vases
- Sizing advice: vase about one third of total arrangement height; at least 30 to 40 cm for stability with tall plumes.
- Picks cluster on metal sets, wood vases for dry stems, ceramic and bamboo; tall narrow vs short wide changes the look.
- We add pet safety by material, dry-only warnings, weighting the base and neck width vs stem count.
## best-candle-holders
- Guides split by stoneware, tapers, hurricanes, wall sconces and lanterns; styling advice: varied heights, trays, match finishes to hardware.
- Placement ideas: coffee table clusters, tall pieces on mantels, sconces flanking a mirror.
- We add candle-diameter fit, tipping and wax residue, shield vs open flame and wood base fire care.
## best-decorative-trays
- Guides lead with material (walnut, brass, acacia, resin), shape (round, rectangle, square) and size for table; trays let you stage vignettes you can lift away.
- Shape tips: round suits round tables and layering, rectangular suits consoles.
- We add rim height, 2/3 table width rule, material wear (wood rings, resin chips) and drink-safe finishes.
## best-bookends
- Roundups pick overall (wood, heavy-gauge metal, tree-design metal), value (black metal with foam pads, clear four-packs), decorative (marble and wood, geometric), heavy books, vinyl and novelty.
- Material, weight and non-slip pads are the common criteria.
- We add arm length vs tallest volume, shelf scratching, cast iron weight and clear plastic yellowing.
## best-coffee-table-books
- Picks sorted by interest: travel, architecture and design, art, photography, pop culture; designers often stack three; rotate books every few months.
- Display tips: stack similar tones, remove dust jackets for a calmer look.
- We add stack size, weight on the table, spine-out styling and the honesty that a decor book is bought for the cover.
