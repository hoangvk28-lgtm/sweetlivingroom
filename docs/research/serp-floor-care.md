## best-hardwood-floor-cleaners
- Bona appears most often (best overall or best spray); Method Squirt + Mop, Better Life, Murphy Oil Soap and Zep Hardwood & Laminate follow.
- Guides lead with pH-neutral, residue-free and spray-versus-concentrate.
- They warn against vinegar, ammonia and all-purpose cleaners and stress spot testing.
- They split picks by finish type (polyurethane versus hard wax oil) and by large-area versus quick-spray use.
- Gap: oil soap film buildup, polish restorer buildup, and over-dosing concentrates; we cover these.
## best-steam-mops
- Bissell PowerFresh and Shark Steam Pocket lead; Tineco and PurSteam appear for premium and budget.
- Guides lead with floor type, heat-up time, tank size and attachments (pads, garment steamer).
- They split picks for pets, hardwood and tile with grout.
- Hardwood advice conflicts: NWFA says never, some retailers say low steam is fine.
- Gap: honest hardwood warning and pad washing; we cover both.
## best-pet-hair-vacuums
- Dyson V15, Shark PowerDetect, Shark TruePet and Bissell Pet Hair Eraser recur; Miele for canister.
- Guides split picks by upright, cordless, canister, handheld and robot, and by budget.
- They lead with bin size, anti-tangle brush rolls, and stairs/upholstery tools.
- They note brush roll wrapping and weaker deep-carpet performance on some cordless units.
- Gap: bare-floor scatter, filter washing schedule and real weight on stairs; we cover these.

## best-lint-rollers-for-furniture
- Reviewers repeatedly name a reusable friction roller as the pet hair pick, noting it costs more up front but produces no waste.
- Sticky sheet rollers are split by width, refill count and stickiness; value multipacks are the budget answer.
- One furniture-focused guide warns that a reusable remover is not suited to smooth surfaces like leather.
- For cat hair on upholstery, guides point to dedicated upholstery tools or a vacuum with a pet attachment.
- Gap: friction versus tape on different fabrics, refill cost over a year, and when a roller is the wrong tool.

## best-handheld-vacuums
- Top guides lead with size, weight, power and runtime, with some suggesting 15 air watts or more as a floor for real cleaning.
- Corded versus cordless and runtime are flagged by home-focused reviewers; pet hair motorized brush tools are a separate pick.
- Picks split into best overall, best value cordless, pet hair, car and premium categories.
- Several guides are dated 2022 to 2023, so current model coverage is thin.
- Gap: peak Pa claims on cheap units versus air watts, wall mount charging, and dust cup size honesty.

## best-leather-conditioners-for-sofas
- Guides name Leather Honey, Bickmore, Furniture Clinic, Lexol and Chemical Guys; advice is to use oil-based conditioners without waxes or silicones, avoid mink oil, add UV protection for sunny rooms and check the product suits finished upholstery leather.
- Gap: pigmented versus aniline leather tolerance, patch testing, and that faux leather needs no conditioner.

## best-fabric-shavers
- Guides name Conair, AlwaysLux, Steamery and Electrolux; electric shavers with adjustable height suit thick fabrics and manual ones give control on delicate items.
- Gap: upholstery-specific use (couch, blankets), blade guard and cut depth, battery runtime and fabric damage risk.
