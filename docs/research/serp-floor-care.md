## best-hardwood-floor-cleaners
- Bona appears most often (best overall or best spray); Method Squirt + Mop, Better Life, Murphy Oil Soap and Zep Hardwood & Laminate follow.
- Guides lead with pH-neutral, residue-free and spray-versus-concentrate.
- They warn against vinegar, ammonia and all-purpose cleaners and stress spot testing.
- They split picks by finish type (polyurethane versus hard wax oil) and by large-area versus quick-spray use.
- Gap: oil soap film buildup, polish restorer buildup, and over-dosing concentrates; we cover these.
## best-steam-mops
- Bissell PowerFresh and Shark Steam Pocket lead; Tineco and PurSteam appear for premium and budget.
- Guides lead with floor type, heat-up time, tank size and attachments (pads, garment steamer).
- They split picks for pets, hardwood and tile with grout.
- Hardwood advice conflicts: NWFA says never, some retailers say low steam is fine.
- Gap: honest hardwood warning and pad washing; we cover both.
## best-pet-hair-vacuums
- Dyson V15, Shark PowerDetect, Shark TruePet and Bissell Pet Hair Eraser recur; Miele for canister.
- Guides split picks by upright, cordless, canister, handheld and robot, and by budget.
- They lead with bin size, anti-tangle brush rolls, and stairs/upholstery tools.
- They note brush roll wrapping and weaker deep-carpet performance on some cordless units.
- Gap: bare-floor scatter, filter washing schedule and real weight on stairs; we cover these.
