## best-wall-mounted-electric-fireplaces

## best-rattan-coffee-tables

- Gap: natural versus PE and PVC faux rattan, glass-top safety, and shelf load limits.

## best-travertine-coffee-tables
- Results lean on vintage and designer stone tables and flag that real travertine is porous and needs sealing; budget faux tables are a separate class.
- Gap: honest labelling of travertine-look veneer versus stone, hollow-core weight and static load limits.

## best-live-edge-coffee-tables

## best-acrylic-coffee-tables
- Editorial lists (some dated) name waterfall, round and nesting acrylic tables; sources say acrylic is lighter and tougher than glass but scratches more easily.
- Buying advice: sturdiness, easy cleaning, size and shape; thickness around 0.75 in is common in listings.
- Gap: scratch care, yellowing and fingerprints, thickness numbers and edge polish.

## best-concrete-coffee-tables
- Roundups and retailers stress sturdiness and temperature resistance; real concrete tables weigh roughly 80 to 150 lb, with fiber-reinforced versions lighter.
- Gap: real concrete versus MgO and fiberglass stone-look tables, floor load in apartments and sealing.

## best-tv-stands-for-43-inch-tvs

- Gap: per-shelf load, arch top versus usable top shelf height, wall anchoring and doors versus open shelves.
