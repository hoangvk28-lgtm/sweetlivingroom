## best-moroccan-rugs
- Top guides split into authentic handwoven (Beni Ourain, kilim, boucherouite) and machine-made washable options.
- They lead with handwoven vs machine-made, pile and shedding; named brands recur (SAFAVIEH, JONATHAN Y).
- Pet-friendly and low-shedding framing is common for machine-made rugs.
- Gap: be explicit that these are Moroccan-style, and that washers fit only about 5x7; we state both.
## best-sisal-rugs
- Top guides name a SAFAVIEH Natural Fiber 8x10 as best overall; budget picks with non-slip backing; 6x9 for dining.
- They lead with size by room, backing, durability, and warn sisal soaks up spills.
- Some results blur sisal with jute; wool and sisal blends are cited for durability.
- Gap: coarse feel for bare feet, creases cracking and jute blends wearing faster; we cover these.
## best-faux-sheepskin-rugs
- Top guides pick by size and use: large plush, pet-friendly weave, runner for benches, round, pelt shapes.
- They lead with pile depth, suede or rubber non-slip backing and machine washability.
- Pelt and runner shapes appear; faux fur vs real is discussed.
- Gap: matting, melt-from-heat and seam strength; we cover all three and say real sheepskin lasts longer.

## best-cotton-rugs
- Search returned retailer pages and a few roundups; consistent points: cotton is often machine washable, durable and rot-resistant.
- Larger cotton rugs usually need spot or professional cleaning; blends with polypropylene add durability.
- Practical picks are flat-woven or braided; categories by style, colour and size.
- Gap: sliding, flattening in traffic paths and slow drying; we cover all three.
## best-braided-rugs
- Top guides pick by fibre: polypropylene for durability and stains, cotton for softness, jute for farmhouse natural look.
- They lead with material, indoor-outdoor use and machine washability; named picks include a Safavieh Adirondack and a nuLOOM braided.
- Jute versions cannot be machine washed; cotton flattens faster in traffic paths.
- Gap: real braid vs printed braided-style; we state which is which for each pick.

## best-hand-knotted-rugs
- Search returned mostly retailer pages; consistent points: hand-knotted is denser, longer lasting and holds finer detail than tufted.
- Authenticity test is flipping the rug (no glued backing); wool is the usual fibre; Persian and Turkish styles dominate.
- Brands named include Loloi, Nourison and Safavieh.
- Gap: honest notes on early shedding, specialist cleaning and thin documentation on unbranded listings; we cover these.
